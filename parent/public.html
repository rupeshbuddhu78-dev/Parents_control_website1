Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Parental Control

Is it legal to use this application?
Yes, it is completely legal to use Parental Control to monitor minor children's devices. Parents have the legal right to monitor their children's online activity for safety purposes.
How is my data protected?
We use enterprise-grade AES-256 encryption for all data transmission and storage. Your family's data is completely private and secure. We never share or sell your data to third parties.
What devices are compatible?
Parental Control works on Android 5.0+ devices. The parent dashboard works on any device with a web browser — Android, iOS, Windows, or Mac.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with no questions asked. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees.
How do I install on the target device?
Simply download the app, install it on the child's device, login to your dashboard, and enter the unique pairing code. The entire setup takes less than 3 minutes.
